# Competitions

## Kaggle Competitions Overview
Gayathri has participated in several Kaggle competitions, primarily as a way to learn by building rather than to chase leaderboard placement. She has competed in 5 competitions solo and 2 as part of a team. Her Kaggle profile: https://www.kaggle.com/gayathrirajeev77

## Orbit Wars
A real-time strategy simulation competition (4,729 teams) where players conquer planets rotating around a sun in continuous 2D space. Competed with a teammate.

## The 2026 NeuroGolf Championship
A research competition (2,963 teams) focused on designing the smallest neural networks to solve ARC-AGI image transformation tasks.

## NVIDIA Nemotron Model Reasoning Challenge
A competition (4,182 teams) focused on advancing reasoning techniques using NVIDIA Nemotron open models on a novel benchmark.

## Pokémon TCG AI Battle Challenge
A simulation competition (6,807 teams) building an AI training agent to play the Pokémon Trading Card Game. Competed with a teammate.

## Maze Crawler
A playground simulation competition (459 teams) navigating an infinite scrolling maze with fog of war in a 1v1 format.

## Neural Debris Removal in Streak Detection Models
A community competition (567 teams), part of the European Space Agency's "Secure Your AI" series.
